Reuse Public Kirtan Data

Verified kirtan text, translations, images, and database exports are open for respectful public use.

PushtiKirtan.com exists as seva: to preserve, organize, and pass forward the priceless dharohar of Pushtimargiya kirtans for Vaishnavs today and for future generations. The public corpus is shared without monetary intent so readers, YouTube and video creators, other content creators, website publishers, researchers, developers, educators, and community projects can study, copy, adapt, and reuse verified kirtan data while honoring the dignity of Pushtimarg and the Vaishnav tradition.

What You May Reuse

You may reuse the verified public kirtan text, AI-assisted English meanings, transliterations, raag and occasion metadata, AI-generated images, and sanitized public database exports in videos, YouTube descriptions, educational content, websites, apps, search tools, archives, research, and other creator or development projects, subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, and cautions on this page and in the project repository.

The images were generated using kirtan descriptions. The English translations and transliterations were prepared with AI assistance and then reviewed through the project workflow. The Hindi/Vraj/Braj Bhasha kirtan text is spell checked by humans before a kirtan is marked verified.

This permission is offered in good faith to support preservation, learning, accessibility, and devotional use. It is not a certification that any material is complete, error-free, authoritative, lineage-approved, suitable for every use, or free from third-party rights or community concerns.

Verified Kirtans Only

Only verified kirtans may be reused, republished, indexed, quoted as reliable, incorporated into another public archive, or otherwise presented to the public as part of the PushtiKirtan.com public corpus. In the database, this means the verified column in tbl_kirtan must be true / 1.

If verified is false / 0, the record is draft, working, incomplete, or unverified material. It must not be used publicly because it may contain unreviewed text, incomplete translation, spelling issues, incorrect metadata, formatting problems, attribution issues, or other errors.

Anyone downloading or reusing the database is solely responsible for filtering out unverified records before publication, redistribution, indexing, display, training, analysis, or any other public or private downstream use.

Please preserve the dignity, honor, and devotional character of Pushtimarg, Shri Krishna, Shri Vallabh, Shri Gusainji, Shrinathji, the Ashtasakha and Ashtachhap poets, and the Vaishnav tradition.

Do not present altered, incomplete, unverified, draft, or machine-generated material as authoritative scriptural, liturgical, scholarly, historical, or lineage-approved text. If you modify, translate, summarize, reorganize, excerpt, combine, or otherwise adapt the material, clearly state that changes were made and do not imply endorsement by PushtiKirtan.com, its maintainers, any Vaishnav community, any temple, any lineage, or any religious authority.

Reuse should be guided by bhav, maryada, humility, and care. The purpose of sharing this corpus is preservation and propagation of a sacred literary heritage, not commercial exploitation, sensational presentation, disrespectful remixing, or uses that diminish the devotional nature of the material.
